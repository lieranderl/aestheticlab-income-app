## Global Constraints
- Money is int64 cents; every formula lives in internal/domain. Effective terms, strict bonus threshold, manual-override exclusion, largest-remainder dividends, confirmed-only balances and certificate rules remain unchanged.
- Financial writes append audit in the same transaction; read before write, void never delete, closed months locked even for admin. Only admin settles, closes and reopens. Employee handlers receive only their own financial data.
- Russian only, gender-neutral glossary; 375 px no overflow, ≥44 px targets, ≥16 px inputs, both themes. Native dialogs, semantic daisyUI tokens, no inline style/custom JS. Datastar and plain HTML fallback. Never interpolate user data into expressions.
- Financial POSTs never automatically retry. Reuse sendFinancialForm, response-unknown notice, busy/readiness guards and sticky FinancialActionRow. Failed forms preserve values and show actionable Russian feedback.
- Production only, October test month on main database. Do not change main data/config, add fake production records, recreate trial or execute cleanup. Tests use memory/emulator with injected dates. Controller handles docs, independent reviews, PR/merge/deploy. Every review issue must be fixed before merge, including minor findings.

## Review Focus
- Selected old month must exclude later financial rows; a closed month must read frozen result/terms, even after new terms or payments.
- Open month estimates must not become actual salary/dividends until close; staff cannot see anyone else's snapshot or salon figures.
- Repeated/stale payment or close/reopen actions must not blindly repeat an old suggested amount or mutate newly changed month state.
- Missing historical reporters use audited status on each day, including created/disabled/reactivated users. Missing reports and stale recount are warnings; pending rows/month order/running month are blockers.
- Carry/opening rows, self bonuses, voided old accruals and month-end dates must not be counted twice in preview, close, reopening or settlement breakdown.
